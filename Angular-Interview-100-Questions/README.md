# Angular Interview Study Pack — 100 Questions

Each question has its own detailed Markdown file. Files are grouped by category and numbered in the original order.

## How to study so you remember

1. Read the **interview-ready answer** and **memory anchor** first.
2. Close the file and explain the concept aloud in your own words.
3. Reopen it and study the internal behavior, banking example, code, comparison, and mistakes.
4. Answer the scenario question without looking at the notes.
5. Revisit the revision card after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, and 14 days.

Do not try to memorize every sentence. Remember the concept through four anchors: **owner, data direction, lifetime, and failure behavior**.
