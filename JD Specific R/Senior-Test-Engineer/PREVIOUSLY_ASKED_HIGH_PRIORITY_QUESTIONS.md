# Previously Asked Senior Test Engineer Questions — High Priority

These questions were previously asked and should receive high priority during interview preparation.

**Priority marker:** `HP` — High Priority

1. **[HP]** Walk me through how you approach testing a new feature.
2. **[HP]** Tell me about your current automation framework and your contribution to it.
3. **[HP]** What have you automated yourself?
4. **[HP]** How do you decide what should and shouldn't be automated?
5. **[HP]** Tell me about a flaky/brittle automation problem you solved.
6. **[HP]** If you inherited a slow and unreliable automation suite, how would you improve it?
7. **[HP]** How do you approach API testing?
8. **[HP]** What do you validate beyond the HTTP status code?
9. **[HP]** How do you approach integration testing when several systems are involved?
10. **[HP]** Tell me about your mobile-testing experience.
11. **[HP]** How is your mobile-testing strategy different from web?
12. **[HP]** How do you perform exploratory testing?
13. **[HP]** Tell me about a serious defect you discovered.
14. **[HP]** Tell me about a production issue you investigated.
15. **[HP]** What do you do when you can't reproduce a production issue?
16. **[HP]** What happens when you disagree with a developer about a defect?
17. **[HP]** How do you test when requirements aren't clear?
18. **[HP]** How do you prioritise testing when you don't have enough time?
19. **[HP]** How do you determine regression scope?
20. **[HP]** How do you decide whether you're comfortable with a release?
21. **[HP]** How do you communicate testing risk to a Product Owner/Test Lead?
22. **[HP]** How do you manage multiple projects or competing priorities?
23. **[HP]** How have you improved QA processes in your team?
24. **[HP]** How do you use Jira/TestRail to manage testing?
25. **[HP]** How do you contribute during refinement/planning rather than waiting for development to finish?
